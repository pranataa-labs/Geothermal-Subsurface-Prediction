🧠 AI Specifications & Geophysical Parameters
Model Architecture (Optuna Tuned)

This system predicts the 3D temperature profile (Multi-Output) using a Hybrid Stacking Ensemble. The AI combines two state-of-the-art algorithms unified by a Meta-Learner:

  • Random Forest (RF): 250 Decision Trees (max_depth: 19).
  • XGBoost: 300 Boosting Trees (max_depth: 11, Learning Rate: 0.072).
  • Meta-Learner: Linear Regression with a rigorous 5-Fold Cross Validation testing scheme.
Feature Selection Justification (Geophysics-Driven)

Out of 119 original OpenEI data attributes (Continental US), the AI filters 10 deepest structural geophysical parameters:

  • Anti Spatial Bias: Spatial coordinate features (Longitude/Latitude) are removed so the AI learns pure physical principles rather than memorizing spatial interpolation.
  • Anti Data Leakage: Shallow temperature features (T_0) are eliminated to prevent the AI from cheating with surface meteorological weather.

*This model is designed as an alternative to mitigate financial risks (capital expenditure) before the physical geothermal drilling operational phase is conducted.
